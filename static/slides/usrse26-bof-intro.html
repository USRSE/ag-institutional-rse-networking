Self-determination theory

People are motivated and thrive when three basic psychological needs are met (Deci & Ryan, 1985)

Autonomy

Having a say in how we work and what we build. Local communities can organize for structural change.

Competence

Feeling effective and growing. Peers offer shared best practices, mentors, and new ideas.

Relatedness

Feeling connected and that we belong. Peers to share successes, frustrations, and interests.
