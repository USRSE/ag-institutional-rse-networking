# Subscribe to the monthly meeting

We meet on the **2nd Tuesday of each month, 1:00-2:00 PM CT**

Subscribing keeps your calendar up to date automatically (link changes, cancellations).

* [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/r?cid=webcal://usrse.github.io/ag-institutional-rse-networking/meeting.ics)
* [Outlook (web)](https://outlook.live.com/calendar/0/addfromweb?url=https%3A%2F%2Fusrse.github.io%2Fag-institutional-rse-networking%2Fmeeting.ics&name=Institutional%20RSE%20Networking%20AG)
* [Apple Calendar / Outlook desktop / other apps](webcal://usrse.github.io/ag-institutional-rse-networking/meeting.ics)

Alternatively, click "Add calendar from URL" in your calendar app and paste:

```
https://usrse.github.io/ag-institutional-rse-networking/meeting.ics
```
